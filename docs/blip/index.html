TR4CE BLIP

a dot-matrix desk companion

TR4CE Blip is a small desktop app for Windows. Blip lives in a corner of the screen, made only of dots, and keeps your notes, to-dos and calendar one click away. It peeks out when something needs your attention and stays out of the way when nothing does.

What it does

  • Notes and to-dos with dates, tags and reminders, typed in plain language.
  • Your Google Calendar events in Blip's agenda, and new events created from it.
  • Optional sync between your own computers through a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive.
  • A play mode with tricks and small games.

Your data

Blip has no servers. Your notes and tasks are stored on your computer. When you connect a Google account, Blip talks directly to Google from your computer and nothing passes through anyone else. Read the privacy policy.
